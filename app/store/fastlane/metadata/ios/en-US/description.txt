Made in Egypt Gate (منصة صنع في مصر) is the unified national B2B platform: a digital platform that connects factories and suppliers across Egypt's 27 governorates with local and international buyers and supports the digital transformation of the industrial sector in a trusted, transparent environment.

The app opens the official website madeinegyptgate.com directly, so everything new on the platform is in the app at once.

In the app:
• The marketplace for Egyptian products: buy wholesale from factories, suppliers and retailers across Egypt's governorates, or request a quote for your quantity.
• The factory and supplier directory: factories and companies by sector, governorate and product, each with its profile and a quote request through the platform.
• Tenders and price requests (RFQs): opportunities published from their sources, with the issuing body, governorate, sector, closing date and the link to the official announcement.
• Projects: public and private projects across Egypt's governorates, their stage, value, the parties carrying them out and the materials needed, with the source link.
• Industrial and free zones, and the five export markets.
• Retail outlet offers, and the media center: industry news, exhibitions and events.
• Janna, the platform's smart assistant: she speaks Egyptian Arabic and English, and you can talk to her on a live video call.
• Bassem, the smart deals advisor, for Core Partner and Strategic Partner members.
• Register your company and manage its profile from the member dashboard.

Permissions:
• The app asks for the microphone only when you start a live call with Janna or Bassem or tap the microphone button in Janna's chat (with speech recognition, to turn what you say into text), and for the camera or photos only when you choose to upload a picture or document.

Owner: Egypt Industrial Development Association

The platform is a directory, an e-marketplace and an information intermediary; it is not a government body or a contracting authority.
