# JANNA AI — Intelligent Digital Human Assistant
## MADE IN EGYPT PLATFORM
> Permanent project reference (development document), supplied by the platform owner. Do not replace the holographic character or change the website design without the owner's approval.

### 1. Project Vision
Transform Janna from a static holographic character into a realistic, intelligent, interactive AI digital employee representing the Made in Egypt platform.
Janna must communicate naturally with visitors, understand their needs, provide accurate information, search the platform database, and guide users through available services.

### 2. Personality and Identity
- Name: Janna.
- Position: Official AI Digital Assistant of Made in Egypt.
- Gender: Female digital human.
- Default language: Egyptian Arabic.
- Additional languages: English and Modern Standard Arabic.
- Personality: Friendly, professional, intelligent, welcoming, and confident.

Janna must sound and behave like a real Egyptian female employee.

### 3. Voice and Conversation
Implement:
- Native Egyptian Arabic female voice.
- Natural human-like pronunciation.
- Emotion-aware voice synthesis.
- Real-time voice conversations.
- Automatic speech recognition.
- Voice activity detection.
- Real-time interruption support.
- Natural conversational pauses.
- Context-aware responses.
- Arabic and English language switching.

Avoid robotic or generic Arabic voices.

### 4. Digital Human Animation
Enhance the existing holographic character with:
- Accurate lip synchronization.
- Natural facial expressions.
- Eye movement and blinking.
- Head movement.
- Listening animation.
- Speaking animation.
- Thinking animation.
- Welcoming and smiling expressions.

Preserve the existing character design and website identity.

### 5. Complete Knowledge Base
Create a centralized knowledge base containing:
1. About Made in Egypt.
2. Platform vision and mission.
3. Platform services.
4. Factory registration.
5. Membership packages.
6. Factory profiles.
7. Industrial sectors.
8. Product marketplace.
9. Export services.
10. International trade.
11. Investment opportunities.
12. Business matchmaking.
13. Suppliers and buyers.
14. Tenders and projects.
15. Training and employment.
16. Customer support.
17. Frequently asked questions.
18. Terms and conditions.
19. Privacy policies.
20. Registration and payment procedures.

All information must be based on verified official platform content.

### 6. Live Database Integration
Connect Janna directly to the existing Made in Egypt database through secure APIs.
She must be able to search and retrieve:
- Registered factories.
- Factory names and profiles.
- Industrial activities.
- Factory locations.
- Product listings.
- Product specifications.
- Published prices.
- Available suppliers.
- Authorized contact information.
- Investment opportunities.
- Export opportunities.
- Business requests.

Example: Visitor: "I am looking for plastic manufacturers in 10th of Ramadan City." Janna should search the actual database and display matching factory profiles.
Never invent factory information, prices, availability, or statistics.

### 7. Visitor Identification
Janna should recognize visitor categories: factory owners, manufacturers, importers, exporters, investors, suppliers, distributors, traders, job seekers, general visitors.
She must adapt her responses according to each visitor's needs.

### 8. Interactive Website Functions
Janna should be able to:
- Navigate users to factory registration.
- Search factories and products.
- Display factory profiles.
- Open industrial categories.
- Direct visitors to membership services.
- Access export and investment sections.
- Guide users to customer support.

Sensitive actions must require user confirmation.

### 9. Conversational Memory
Maintain contextual understanding during active conversations.
Example: Visitor: "I need food factories." Janna: "Which city are you interested in?" Visitor: "Cairo." Janna must understand that Cairo refers to the location of the previously requested food factories.
Avoid repeating questions already answered.

### 10. Technical Architecture
Recommended architecture: Digital Human Engine · Real-Time Voice Engine · Speech-to-Text · Text-to-Speech · AI Conversation Engine · RAG Knowledge Base · Platform Database API · Secure Integration Layer · Analytics Dashboard.

Suggested technologies:
- Avatar: Anam AI or equivalent.
- Voice: Awtaar or equivalent Egyptian Arabic voice provider.
- AI: Gemini Live API or equivalent.
- Knowledge: RAG architecture.
- Database: Existing Made in Egypt infrastructure.

### 11. Security
- Secure server-side API integration.
- No exposed API keys.
- Role-based access control.
- Protection of confidential factory data.
- Privacy-compliant conversation storage.
- Human support escalation.
- Verified information only.

### 12. Analytics
Create an administration dashboard showing: total conversations, daily interactions, most searched factories, most searched products, popular industrial sectors, registration referrals, unanswered questions, user satisfaction, Arabic and English usage.

### 13. Development Phases
- Phase 1: Realistic avatar and Egyptian voice.
- Phase 2: Knowledge base and conversational intelligence.
- Phase 3: Live database and interactive services.
- Phase 4: Analytics, optimization, and final testing.

### FINAL REQUIREMENT
Janna must become a fully integrated AI digital employee of Made in Egypt.
The final experience must combine:
REALISTIC DIGITAL HUMAN + NATIVE EGYPTIAN VOICE + REAL-TIME CONVERSATION + VERIFIED PLATFORM KNOWLEDGE + LIVE DATABASE ACCESS + INTERACTIVE WEBSITE SERVICES.

Before modifying the existing project, inspect its source code, architecture, database, and current holographic implementation.
Preserve all existing website functionality and visual identity.
