# PART ONE: GENERAL PROJECT VISION

## 1. Strategic Objective

Transform GCC Tenders Gate from a conventional digital tenders and procurement information platform into an integrated AI-powered business development and advisory ecosystem.

The platform must provide two dedicated AI digital human personalities:

JANNA: Responsible for attracting visitors, explaining platform benefits, marketing memberships, assisting with registration and converting visitors into subscribers.

BASSEM: Responsible for supporting registered and eligible subscribed customers as their dedicated Personal AI Business Development Consultant for 365 days.

The objective is to provide customers with continuous business development support, including opportunity discovery, tender intelligence, RFQ monitoring, project tracking, procurement intelligence, business matching, correspondence management and deal follow-up.

The customer must feel that they have more than a platform subscription. They have a dedicated digital business consultant working alongside them.

## 2. IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT INSTRUCTION

Before modifying the current website:

- Inspect the complete existing project.
- Understand the current frontend and backend architecture.
- Identify the existing hologram/avatar implementation.
- Inspect the authentication and registration system.
- Understand the existing membership and payment workflows.
- Inspect the tender and project databases.
- Identify existing APIs and integrations.
- Preserve all existing website functions and design elements.

Do not rebuild the website from scratch.

Integrate JANNA and BASSEM into the existing architecture.

Do not use fabricated database records or demonstration statistics as real platform information.

## رابعاً: عندي 3 قرارات استراتيجية مهمة أنصحك تعتمدها

**1. باسم لازم يكون استباقياً وليس تفاعلياً فقط.**

يعني لا ينتظر العميل يسأله عن المناقصات. هو الذي يكتشف الفرص ويتواصل مع العميل ويقترح الخطوة التالية.

**2. محرك الفرص يكون مركزياً.**

بدلاً من أن يبحث باسم لكل عميل بشكل منفصل، نجمع الفرص مرة واحدة ونحللها ونوزعها حسب نشاط كل شركة. ده يقلل التكلفة ويحسن سرعة الأداء.

**3. لا نعد العميل بالفوز بالمناقصات.**

نعده بخدمة تطوير أعمال ومراقبة فرص ومطابقة ومتابعة وتواصل احترافي. أما الفوز والتعاقد فيعتمدان على شروط الجهة وقرارها وقدرات الشركة.
